Everything built over the last eleven weeks — truth, structure, scenes,
clues, puzzles, checks, NPCs, and now playtest revisions — has to come
together into something another Keeper could pick up and actually run.
This studio is the final assembly pass, tested the only way that matters:
by handing it to someone else.

## Before the session

Bring your revised draft, incorporating whatever the playtest surfaced —
this session assumes week 11 already changed something.

## In the session

Swap your assembled packet with a partner. They pick any scene and try to
answer, using only what's on the page, "what happens here and what would
I need to run it?" Wherever they get stuck or have to guess, that's a gap
between what's in your head and what's actually written down.

## Afterwards

Close those gaps before submitting the Final Scenario. A scenario only you
can run isn't finished — it's still notes.
