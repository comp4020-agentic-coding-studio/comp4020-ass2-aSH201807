# Policies and support

## Late work and extensions

Work submitted late without a prior extension loses 5% of the available
mark per day, up to five days, after which it is not accepted. If
something outside your control is going to make a deadline unworkable,
email Marisol before the deadline, not after — extensions are much easier
to grant in advance than to arrange retroactively.

## Academic integrity

Your scenario, your NPCs, your clue web and your prose need to be your own
work. Using a published scenario's plot, locations or puzzles as your
submission is not acceptable; being inspired by how a published scenario
handles a technique, and saying so, is exactly what this course asks you
to do. If you use an AI tool for drafting or feedback, declare it and say
how, the same way you'd declare a co-writer.

## The Assessment 3 playtest requirement

Assessment 3 requires an actual playtest with a real player — a solo
read-through does not satisfy it. If you don't have a regular group,
contact Idris before week 11: he keeps a standing list matching students
who need a playtest partner with each other.

## Getting help

Bring scenario-specific problems — a stuck clue web, an NPC that isn't
working, a structure that's fighting you — to a studio session or Idris's
office hours. Email Marisol for anything about the course itself: the
brief, the schedule, or an extension request.
