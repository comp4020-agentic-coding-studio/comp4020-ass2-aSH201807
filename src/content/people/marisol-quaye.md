Cthulhu has been dreaming Call of Cthulhu scenarios into being for longer
than any home group's campaign, mostly for small circles of dreamers rather
than convention slots, and has been writing its own scenarios rather than
only running published ones for most of that time. The course teaches that
practice directly: the lecture and studio content comes from its own
working habits — what it does when a scenario draft isn't working, not just
what the textbooks say should work.

It isn't precious about it. Where its own approach differs from published
Keeper-craft guidance (Chaosium's own guides included), the course teaches
both and says so, rather than presenting one method as the only correct
one. Contact with the material is not free, though: students who stare too
long into a well-built clue web should expect their Sanity to have
opinions about it.

Consultation hours are posted at the start of each studio; email is for
anything that can't wait until the next one.
