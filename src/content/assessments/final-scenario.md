## The brief

> Submit the finished, playtested scenario: everything a Keeper who has
> never spoken to you would need to run it.

This is the semester's whole pipeline arriving in one place: premise,
truth and antagonist, structure, scenes, clue web, puzzles, checks, NPCs,
atmosphere, and — critically — the revisions your Assessment 3 playtest
surfaced. A scenario that reads well but ignores what the playtest found
broken is not a finished scenario by this course's standard.

## What you submit

The complete packet: scenario text organised by scene, clue map, NPC
sheets, and any player handouts, assembled so someone else could pick it
up and run it without you in the room. Include a short note naming at
least one specific change you made because of your playtest.

For a worked example of what a finished packet feels like at the table,
see the [Play](/play/) demo scenario.
