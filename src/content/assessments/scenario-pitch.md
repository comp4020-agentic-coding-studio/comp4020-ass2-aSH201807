## The brief

> State your premise, your antagonist, and the causal chain — goal, past
> action, next step, consequence if unopposed — that your scenario hangs on.

This is the studio work from weeks 1–3 written up formally: the raw idea,
the Mythos flavour it leans on, the specific entity or faction fused into
it, and the truth stated as cause and effect rather than atmosphere. Add a
short closing note on the kind of horror and player experience you're
aiming for — cosmic dread, creeping wrongness, body horror, paranoia — so
your marker knows what you're steering toward before structure gets
decided in week 4.

## What you submit

One to two pages: premise paragraph, named antagonist, truth as a four-step
chain, and the horror/player-experience note. No scenes, clues, or NPCs
yet — those come later and this pitch is what they get built on.
