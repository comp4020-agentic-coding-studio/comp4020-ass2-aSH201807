# This is not a rules course

It's not Mythos lore either.

It's what a Keeper actually does to turn an idea into a runnable scenario.

---

## Five decisions, in order

1. Premise — a spark, made specific
2. Truth & antagonist — what actually happened, and why
3. Structure — linear or open, committed to early
4. Clues, puzzles, checks, NPCs — built to survive a bad roll
5. Playtest & assembly — tested on someone else, handed to someone else

---

{/* _class: quote */}

> The scenario isn't finished when it reads well.
> It's finished when someone else can run it.
