The bus lets you off at the top of the hill, and Blackwater Cove spreads
out below: a scatter of grey roofs, a stone breakwater, and — dominating
the waterfront — a clock tower whose big iron face is visibly running
backwards. Nobody in the cove will meet your eye about it.

The harbourmaster, Aldous Penrose, has not been seen in six days. The
constable's report is two lines long and says nothing. His office is
still unlocked; the tavern by the docks is not.
