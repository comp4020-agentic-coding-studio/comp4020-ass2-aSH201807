# Materials

These are the external references the course draws on — the full working
source list, not just the handful cited by name elsewhere on the site. Every
entry below has been individually verified: its link resolves, and its
author/venue/content match what's claimed about it. (The course author's own
original notes, which the studios and lectures also draw on directly, are a
private working file rather than a public source and so aren't listed here.)

## 1. Call of Cthulhu 7th Edition Quick-Start Rules

Chaosium. Includes the introductory scenario "The Haunting," cited in
[Studio 1](/sessions/01-what-is-a-scenario/).

[chaosium.itch.io/call-of-cthulhu-quickstart-rules](https://chaosium.itch.io/call-of-cthulhu-quickstart-rules)

## 2. Call of Cthulhu TRPG Scenario Writing Guidelines (Autumn 2024 Edition)

KADOKAWA / official Japanese CoC line ("新クトゥルフ神話TRPG"). A
formatting/structure style guide covering required sections, line-count
limits, and bracket conventions. In Japanese.

[note.com/cthulhumasters/n/n67c2b58b7ece](https://note.com/cthulhumasters/n/n67c2b58b7ece)

## 3. "Create a Scenario" masterclass, Chaosium Con Australia 2024

Mike Mason, Chaosium Creative Director.

[chaosium.com — Create a Scenario masterclass](https://www.chaosium.com/blogcreate-a-scenario-watch-call-of-cthulhu-creative-director-mike-masons-masterclass-at-chaosium-con-australia/)

## 4. Notes on Writing Horror Scenarios the Sandy Petersen Way

The Alexandrian (Justin Alexander, 2018), summarizing a Sandy Petersen
seminar. Source of M.R. James's Three Rules, the Scenario Seed, the Scenario
Spine, and the Creepy Stuff Rule, cited in
[Studio 10](/sessions/10-atmosphere-and-escalation/).

[thealexandrian.net/wordpress/40658/roleplaying-games/notes-on-writing-horror-scenarios-the-sandy-petersen-way](https://thealexandrian.net/wordpress/40658/roleplaying-games/notes-on-writing-horror-scenarios-the-sandy-petersen-way)

## 5. Call of Cthulhu 7e Scenario Design Guide

Andrew Blase, The Role Players Guild. Source of the hidden-truth
six-question framework and the core/supporting/advantage clue-web model,
cited in [Studio 1](/sessions/01-what-is-a-scenario/).

[theroleplayersguild.com/articles/coc-7e/keeper-resources/scenario-design](https://theroleplayersguild.com/articles/coc-7e/keeper-resources/scenario-design)

## 6. Call of Cthulhu 7e Keeper Resources Guide

The Role Players Guild. The hub page linking out to guide 5 above plus the
running/NPC/pacing guide below.

[theroleplayersguild.com/articles/coc-7e/keeper-resources](https://theroleplayersguild.com/articles/coc-7e/keeper-resources)

## 7. Learning from Ludemes: An Inventory of Common Player Actions within TTRPGs to Inform Principled Design of Game-Based Learning Experiences

Riel & Monahan, 2024. International Journal of Role-Playing, No. 15.
Peer-reviewed, CC BY 4.0; catalogs player actions ("ludemes") in D&D 5e and
links them to learning-process factors.

[journals.uu.se/IJRP/article/view/325](https://journals.uu.se/IJRP/article/view/325)

## 8. Tabletop Roleplaying Games as Procedural Content Generators

Guzdial, Acharya, Kreminski, Cook, Eladhari, Liapis, Sullivan. FDG '20. A
free PDF of this paper is available via arXiv.

[doi.org/10.1145/3402942.3409605](https://doi.org/10.1145/3402942.3409605)
· [arXiv:2007.06108 (free PDF)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2007.06108)

## 9. Dread Trident: Tabletop Role-Playing Games and the Modern Fantastic — ch. 5, "Lovecraft's (Cthulhu) Mythos"

Curtis D. Carbonell. Liverpool University Press, 2019. Abstract and
structure visible; full text paywalled.

[academic.oup.com — chapter abstract](https://academic.oup.com/liverpool-scholarship-online/book/34021/chapter-abstract/288927075)

## 10, 11 & 13. How to Run Call of Cthulhu 7e: Keeper Guide to Sessions, NPCs, and Campaign Pacing

Andrew Blase, The Role Players Guild. One combined guide covering running a
session, building NPCs (goal/fear/resource/limit/change-trigger), and
pacing a campaign arc (discovery/danger/decision/recovery, "revelation
clocks"). Previously catalogued as three separate articles; the site has
since merged them into sections of this one page.

[theroleplayersguild.com/articles/coc-7e/keeper-resources/running-coc-guide](https://theroleplayersguild.com/articles/coc-7e/keeper-resources/running-coc-guide)

## 12. Horror Atmosphere Guide (CoC 7e)

Andrew Blase, The Role Players Guild. Source of the five-stage escalation
model (unease, confirmation, encroachment, confrontation, aftermath), cited
in [Studio 10](/sessions/10-atmosphere-and-escalation/).

[theroleplayersguild.com/articles/coc-7e/keeper-resources/horror-atmosphere](https://theroleplayersguild.com/articles/coc-7e/keeper-resources/horror-atmosphere)
