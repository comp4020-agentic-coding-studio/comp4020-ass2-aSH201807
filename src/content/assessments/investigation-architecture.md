## The brief

> Submit the complete skeleton of your scenario: every scene, every clue,
> where puzzles and checks sit, and who your NPCs are and why.

This is the studio work from weeks 4–9 assembled into one document: your
committed structure, your scene map, your clue web tagged against both
taxonomies from week 6, your puzzle placements, your reworked checks, and
your cut-and-sharpened NPC roster. Nothing needs finished prose yet — this
is graded as engineering, not writing. What matters is whether the pieces
connect: whether a player who fails a roll or misses a clue still has a
way forward.

## What you submit

A single document (or linked set of documents) covering: scene map,
clue web with taxonomy tags, puzzle list, check list with failure text,
and NPC roster with one-sentence purposes. This is also the document your
playtest in week 11 will be run from, so it should be usable at a table,
not just readable.
