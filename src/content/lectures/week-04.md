Structure is the decision that's hardest to change later, so it's worth
making deliberately rather than by default. This lecture lays out the
trade-off in plain terms and connects it back to what a truth from week 3
actually needs.

## Outline

- linear structure: easier to run and to prep, less tolerant of player
  detours
- open structure: more resilient to player choice, needs more prepared
  material and improvisation to hold together
- matching structure to your truth: does the story unfold regardless of
  the players, or does it need a specific order to make sense
- hooks: pulled in versus choosing to investigate, and what each implies
  about your players' agency from scene one
- why a structure often needs one scene that exists purely to give a
  non-combat, non-searching character something to do — a social clue
  nobody would otherwise get a scene to use
