A structure is a shape; scenes are what actually gets built inside it. A
short scenario can run on as few as three or four scenes as long as each
one earns its place — a location that provides no new information and no
new pressure is decoration, not a scene.

## Before the session

List every location your truth and structure imply — where the antagonist
has been, where evidence still exists, where a confrontation could happen.
Don't detail them yet.

## In the session

One card per node: what's visible on arrival, what a thorough search
reveals, and what changes if the players stay too long or make noise.
Arrange the cards to check the chain actually connects — a card nobody can
reach from another card is a scene that will never get used.

## Afterwards

This map is what week 6 and 7 attach clues and puzzles to. A thin map now
means thin content later, so it's worth spending real time on before
moving on.
