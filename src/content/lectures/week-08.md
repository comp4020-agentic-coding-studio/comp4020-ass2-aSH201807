A check that can delete the only route through a scene is a trap the
Keeper set for themselves, not a test of the player. This lecture sets
out a simple rule for every check in a scenario, and applies it to Sanity
specifically, where it's most often gotten wrong.

## Outline

- the rule: if this check fails, the player should still be able to
  proceed — just more slowly, at more cost, or with less certainty
- writing failure text that describes what a character notices or does,
  instead of a flat "you find nothing"
- Sanity as a consequence of what a character just witnessed, not a
  target the Keeper is trying to hit
- diegetic wording: phrasing a check as something the character is doing
  in the world, not a mechanical prompt
