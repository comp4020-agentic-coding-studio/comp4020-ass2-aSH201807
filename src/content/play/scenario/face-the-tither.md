title: What Answers
ending: ambiguous
---

You walk out to the end of the breakwater instead, past the point where
the stones are still dry. Whatever is holding its shape in the water
doesn't come any closer, and it doesn't need to — it simply waits for
you to be close enough, and you find you already are.

You don't remember agreeing to anything. You do remember the clock
striking the correct hour behind you, on time, for the first time in
forty years — and Corwin, from the shore, not looking especially
relieved.

You walk back into Blackwater Cove under your own power, and nothing
about you looks any different. But the dockhand at the Gull and Anchor
goes quiet when you order a drink, the way he went quiet about Penrose,
and you find you already know why, the same way you already knew to
come out here tonight.

**The end.**
