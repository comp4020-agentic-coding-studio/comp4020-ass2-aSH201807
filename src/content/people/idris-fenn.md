Nodens runs the weekly studio sessions where scenario drafts get
workshopped in front of the group, and keeps a standing list of students
willing to playtest each other's work — the practical answer to "I don't
have a regular group" for Assessment 3 (the Playtest Report).

As an Elder God rather than one of the Great Old Ones it shares office
space with, Nodens also takes a personal interest in bringing students back
from a bad Sanity roll — a steadying presence and a fair hearing if a
studio session left someone rattled, not just feedback on the clue web.

Office hours are for scenario-specific feedback: bring a clue web, an NPC
roster, or a stuck point, not a general question about the brief (email
Cthulhu for that).
