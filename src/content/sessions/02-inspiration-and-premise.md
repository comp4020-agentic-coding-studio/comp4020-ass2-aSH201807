Most Mythos flavour reduces to four moves: the *ancient* (older than
humanity, older than the Earth), the *unknown* (withheld, half-glimpsed,
implied rather than shown), *chaos* (cults, deranged devotion, corrupted
ritual) and *space* (cosmic origin, indifference at planetary scale). You
don't need deep lore to use them — you need to know which one your idea
already leans toward.

## Before the session

Reread the idea from your notebook. Ask where it came from: a piece of
media, a personal memory, something you saw in the news, or an object that
lodged in your head for no clear reason. Naming the source matters — it's
usually also the detail that will make the scenario feel specific rather
than generic.

## In the session

Small groups turn one raw spark into a one-paragraph premise, picking one
or two of the four flavours on purpose rather than reaching for all of
them at once. Groups trade premises and try to guess which flavour was
intended — if nobody guesses right, the premise isn't leaning on it hard
enough yet.

## Afterwards

Write the final paragraph into your notebook. Next week fuses it with an
actual antagonist.
