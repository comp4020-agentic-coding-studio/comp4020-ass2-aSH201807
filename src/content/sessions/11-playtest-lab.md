A read-through can't tell you where a scenario actually breaks — only a
real player, making real choices you didn't predict, can do that. This
studio is dedicated table time for exactly that, paired with a short list
of what to watch for while it happens.

## Before the session

If you don't already have a group, use the playtest-matching list Nodens
keeps — pairing up with another student's draft is a completely valid way
to satisfy this. Bring your scenario materials in whatever state they're
in; they don't need to be finished, just runnable for a scene or two.

## In the session

Run 60–90 minutes with at least one real player. Keep a running note of:
where they got stuck, any clue or NPC that went completely unused, any
check that felt unnecessary, and any moment that surprised you — a good
surprise is as much useful data as a bad one.

## Afterwards

Write these notes up as the Playtest Report, due this week. Assessment 4
is explicitly graded on whether your final scenario responds to what this
session actually revealed, not just on the design being tidy on paper.
